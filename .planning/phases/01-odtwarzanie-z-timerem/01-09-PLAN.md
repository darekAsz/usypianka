## Phase Goal

**As a** parent, **I want to** choose a built-in noise, set a time and start playback, **so that** the sound fades in gently, loops without audible gaps (also with the screen locked) and fades to silence and stops when the time is up.

*(Story assembled from the ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal clauses; the ROADMAP Goal line itself was not rewritten.)*

**This plan's slice (UAT gap closure):** swiping Usypianka out of recents ends the session gently instead of leaving the noise playing (G-01-2d, user decision D-19), and the notification shows the moon picture on the Xiaomi (G-01-2).

<objective>
Give SessionEngine a "stop, then tell me when silent" operation, use it from a PlaybackService.onTaskRemoved override, and attach moon artwork to the session's MediaMetadata.

Purpose: Closes G-01-2d (root cause: PlaybackService deliberately did not override onTaskRemoved, so the Media3 default keeps a playing service alive; the user decided in UAT that swiping from recents must end the session — recorded as D-19 by plan 01-08) and G-01-2 (root cause: the small icon is set, but MediaMetadata has no artwork; MIUI 12 / Android 11 renders MediaStyle notifications with its own template that shows the artwork and the app name and only puts the small icon in the status bar, so the notification and the lock screen show no picture).
Output: `SessionEngine.stopAndThen` + tests, `PlaybackService.onTaskRemoved`, `renderArtwork()` and artwork in `ExoAudioOutput.startLoop`.
</objective>
